Vocab Box

Learn words and actually remember them.

Vocab Box uses the Leitner system: words you know come back rarely, words you forget come back tomorrow. Reviewing something just before you’d forget it is what moves it into long-term memory.

  • Your words. Paste any list, any language pair. Not someone else’s curriculum.
  • Four ways to study. Learn, review, quiz, and a 60-second match challenge.
  • Private and free. No account, no cost. Your words stay on your device unless you turn on sync.
